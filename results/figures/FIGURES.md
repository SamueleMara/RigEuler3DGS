# Figures

Generated by `generate_paper_figures.py` (Matplotlib, Agg). Synthetic evaluation: 10 scenes, 8 cameras, 60 timestamps, local calibrated multiview setting (not the official FreeArt-21 monocular benchmark). RigEuler3DGS primary run: `v5_canonical`, except `washer_103776` = `v5_gates_canonical`; `v4_bootstrap` appears only in fig09. State errors are reported in each method's native evaluation alignment; degrees and millimetres are never combined.

Scene IDs (tick labels drop the numeric suffix): `foldchair_102255`, `laptop_10211`, `storage_45135`, `oven_101917`, `scissor_11100`, `washer_103776`, `blade_103706`, `fridge_10905`, `stapler_103111`, `USB_100109`.

## fig08_freeart_rendering

## figS3_type_criterion

Files: `supplementary/figS3_type_criterion.*`

Geometric joint-type criterion: distance of the fitted revolute axis from the support centroid divided by the part scale (log axis); dashed line at 10, above which the revolute fit is degenerate and the joint is reported prismatic. Labels give the GT type (a) or the visually expected type (b, c; R/P). Agreement with the visual expectation (not GT accuracy), out of 8: v6 C 5, majority 6, unanimous 5; v5 C 4, majority 3, unanimous 3.

- Plotted fields: rev_axis_radius_over_part_scale (synthetic); v6_/v5_rev_axis_radius_over_part_scale, expected_type (real)
- Units: dimensionless
- N: 10 synthetic; 8 real scenes × 3 arms × 2 versions
